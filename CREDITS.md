# Credits & sources
**Code**: three.js (MIT), Puppeteer (Apache-2.0), FFmpeg.
**Textures** (`assets/textures`): Earth day/night/specular maps and the Moon map come from the three.js examples (MIT repo; planetary imagery derived from NASA data). Moon bump map from jeromeetienne/threex.planets (MIT). The 4096px Moon map is a Lanczos-upscale of the 1K original; the clouds map is re-extracted from the three.js cloud alpha. For a sharper Moon, drop NASA's CGI Moon Kit colour map (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720) at `assets/textures/moon_hires.jpg` (equirectangular JPG, any size) and it is picked up automatically.
**Fonts**: Cinzel and Montserrat (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
**Music**: generated procedurally by `audio/make-music.mjs` (original, no samples, royalty-free).
**Facts on screen** (researched Oct 22, 2026): Old Farmer's Almanac, Farmers' Almanac, NASA Moon site, Space.com, Star Walk, TheSkyLive, Time and Date, CNN/EarthSky (2026 supermoon list).
